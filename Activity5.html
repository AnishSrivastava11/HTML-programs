Importance of Nutrition

Why is Healthy Food Important?

A balanced diet provides our body with the nutrients needed for growth, energy and good health. Eating a variety of fruits, vegetables, grains and proteins helps us stay active and healthy.

Essential Nutrients

  • Carbohydrates – provide energy
  • Proteins – help in growth and repair
  • Vitamins – support body functions
  • Minerals – keep bones and teeth healthy
  • Fats – provide energy and support the body
  • Water – keeps the body hydrated

Healthy Food Ideas

Fruit Salad

Mix fresh apples, bananas, oranges, grapes and pomegranate seeds for a colourful and nutritious snack.

Healthy Breakfast

A healthy breakfast can include oats, fresh fruits, milk or yoghurt and a handful of nuts.

Quick Nutrition Tips

  1. Drink enough water throughout the day.
  2. Eat plenty of fruits and vegetables.
  3. Choose a variety of nutritious foods.
  4. Limit foods that are high in added sugar and salt.
  5. Do not skip regular meals.
